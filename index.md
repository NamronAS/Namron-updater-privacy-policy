# Privacy Policy – Namron Stove Guard Updater
Last updated: 12.03.2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the Namron Stove Guard Updater app (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles user data.
The purpose of the App is to update firmware on compatible Namron Komfyrvakt devices (HDU/PCU) using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.
The App does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
The App does not collect or process:

Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
Location data
Device identifiers
Advertising identifiers
Network information
Usage analytics or telemetry
Crash logs
Any kind of behavioral or technical profiling

The App uses no third‑party analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

## 3. Information the App Does Access
The App requires limited system permissions for technical functionality only.
### 3.1 Bluetooth
Used exclusively to:

Scan for Namron Stove Guard devices
Establish a BLE connection
Perform firmware transfer (DFU)

All communication happens locally between your phone and the hardware device.
No data is saved or sent anywhere.
### 3.2 Location (iOS technical requirement)
Some iOS versions require “Location When In Use” permission to allow BLE scanning.
The App:

Does not request your GPS location
Does not read, store, or transmit location data
Uses this permission only to enable Bluetooth scanning as dictated by iOS restrictions


## 4. How We Handle Data
### 4.1 Local operation
All firmware update operations occur locally on your device.
No information leaves your phone.
### 4.2 No storage
The App stores no data after the update process completes.
### 4.3 No sharing
No information is shared with Namron, Elektroimportøren, third parties, or external systems.

## 5. Third‑Party Services
The App integrates no third‑party:

SDKs
Analytics tools
Tracking frameworks
Cloud services
Ad networks

No external entities receive any information from your device.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
Because the App collects no personal information, it is suitable for users of all ages and complies with Apple’s App Store policies regarding child safety.

## 7. Data Security
Since the App processes no personal data, the risk of data exposure is minimized.
All BLE communication follows secure protocol standards defined by the underlying Bluetooth and DFU frameworks.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary due to:

Technical improvements
Legal requirements
Changes in platform policies

The latest version will always be available via the published URL.
